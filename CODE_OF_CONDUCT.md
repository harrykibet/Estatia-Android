# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers of the Estatia project pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Accepting responsibility, apologizing to those affected, and learning from mistakes.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the people who use the project.
- Showing empathy toward other community members.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing another person's private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission.
- Deliberate intimidation, stalking, or threats.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, including GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and project-related communications. It also applies when an individual is officially representing Estatia or the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issue content, and other contributions that violate it. They may also temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Maintainers are expected to act fairly, consistently, and proportionately, and to communicate the reason for significant enforcement actions when doing so does not compromise the safety or privacy of someone involved.

## Reporting

For conduct concerns, contact the repository maintainers privately through the contact options available on their GitHub profiles. Do not use a public issue or pull request to report harassment or share another person's private information.

Reports will be reviewed and handled as confidentially as practical. Please include enough context to help maintainers understand what happened, where it happened, and who was involved. Do not include unrelated sensitive personal information.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers may use the following responses, depending on the circumstances:

1. **Correction:** A private or public warning explaining the nature of the violation and why the behavior was inappropriate.
2. **Warning:** A formal warning with clear expectations for future conduct and consequences for repeated violations.
3. **Temporary restriction:** A temporary restriction from project spaces or interactions.
4. **Permanent restriction:** A permanent ban from project spaces for repeated violations or severe misconduct.

Maintainers may skip steps when the severity of an incident warrants it. People affected by an enforcement decision may contact the maintainers privately to request a review.
